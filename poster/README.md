## Key discussion points on the poster

1. Does zabiha slaughter improve meat quality, hygiene and safety? *(health)*
2. What makes meat truly zabiha—intention, method and conditions? *(faith/practice)*
3. How do we store, cook and serve meat safely at home? *(health)*
4. How does kindness to animals guide proper zabiha practice? *(faith/practice)*
5. Chicken, beef or goat—what nutrients and portions do we need? *(health)*
6. Which cuts and cooking methods support heart health? *(health)*
7. Fresh, frozen or processed—what should we check on labels and certificates? *(health + certification)*

Extra alternate topics are listed under the poster in `index.html`.
