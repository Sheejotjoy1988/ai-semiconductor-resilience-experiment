\# AI-Based Semiconductor Supply-Chain Resilience



\## Research Title



Predictive Analytics for Semiconductor Supply-Chain Disruption Risk:

An Explainable AI-Based Decision Intelligence Approach



\## Research Focus



This project investigates artificial intelligence and predictive analytics

for semiconductor supply-chain disruption risk and resilience decision-making.



The experiment combines:



\- Semiconductor supply-chain risk

\- Artificial intelligence

\- Machine learning

\- Predictive analytics

\- Explainable AI

\- Decision intelligence

\- Supply-chain resilience



\## Target Journal



Supply Chain Management: An International Journal



\## Research Questions



RQ1: Can machine-learning models predict semiconductor supply-chain disruption risk?



RQ2: Which supply-chain variables contribute most strongly to disruption risk?



RQ3: How can predictive analytics support managerial resilience decisions?

\## Research Limitation



The included dataset is synthetically generated for reproducibility,

pipeline development and pilot computational experimentation.



The synthetic observations must not be represented as observed empirical

semiconductor-industry data.



For journal publication, the framework should subsequently be validated

or extended using appropriate real-world semiconductor, trade, supplier,

logistics or disruption data.
